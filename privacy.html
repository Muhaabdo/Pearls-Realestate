Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Pearls Realestate collects and uses personal data submitted through this website and campaign forms.

Information We Collect

We may collect your name, phone number, email address, selected project, and inquiry details when you submit forms or contact us.

How We Use Your Data

Your data is used to respond to your inquiry, share requested prices and payment plans, and provide relevant project updates. We do not sell your personal data.

Campaign and Analytics Tracking

We use cookies and campaign measurement tools to understand website performance and improve lead quality. Tracking data may include page views, CTA clicks, and form events.

Data Sharing

We may share inquiry details with authorized sales teams and project partners only as needed to provide requested information and follow-up support.

Data Accuracy and Updates

Project details, prices, installment options, and availability can change. Final terms are always based on official developer documentation and reservation contracts.

Your Rights

You can request correction or deletion of your information by contacting us through the Contact page.

Contact

For privacy requests, please use the contact form on our Contact page.

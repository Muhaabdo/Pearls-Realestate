About La Vista Developments

La Vista Developments was founded in 1991 by architects Alaa Hady and Eng. Mohamed Hady. Through more than three decades of delivering exceptional residential and resort communities, La Vista has set a new benchmark for quality living in Egypt.

  • Comprehensive approach: from vision and design to construction, finishing, and operation — all handled in-house.
  • Present in New Cairo, New Capital, El Sherouk, 6th of October, Ain Sokhna, Ras El Hekma, North Coast, and Sheikh Zayed.
  • Notable projects include La Vista City (910 acres, New Capital), El Patio Town, El Patio Oro, and La Vista Ray Ain Sokhna.
  • Committed to balancing privacy and community life, with green spaces making up the majority of every development.
  • Trusted by thousands of clients, with a solid track record of on-time delivery and high design standards.
